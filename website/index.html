Where mathematical structure meets physical evidence.

Four connected studies test a conjectural account of physics through finite-dimensional classical mechanics—carefully separating what is defined, proved, interpreted, and observed.

01 / THESIS

A disciplined conjecture, not a settled identity.

The pilot investigates the founding conjecture that physics studies empirically realized compositional mathematical structures. Mathematical admissibility does not by itself establish physical realization: an empirical correspondence, a declared domain, and an error model are additional data.
