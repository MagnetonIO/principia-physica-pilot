01 / Thesis

Can mechanics become a compositional theory object without confusing equivalence with reality?

The series studies finite-dimensional smooth classical systems, symplectic state spaces, observables, Hamiltonian dynamics, products, interconnection, symmetries, and measurement maps. Field theory, relativity, quantum theories, and new experimental claims remain boundary tests—not conclusions.
