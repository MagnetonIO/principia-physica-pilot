01 / Thesis

Mathematics supplies admissible structures. Physics additionally requires calibrated interfaces, protocols, and empirical warrant.

The pilot keeps these layers explicit. It does not identify mathematical isomorphism with operational equivalence, or mathematical admissibility with physical realization.

The Lean and Haskell artifacts are representative formal companions, not proofs of the complete thesis. They check selected finite algebraic and bookkeeping shadows. They do not formalize the papers’ differential geometry, certify the empirical program, prove the full thesis, or establish physical realization.
