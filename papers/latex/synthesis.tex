\begin{abstract}
This paper synthesises three companion drafts of the PRINCIPIA PHYSICA pilot:
\emph{Compositional Systems in Finite-Dimensional Classical Mechanics}
(hereafter CS), \emph{Hamiltonian Reconstruction as a Physical Theory Object}
(HR), and \emph{Empirical Realization, Equivalence, and Limits} (EL).  A
synthesis is a typed merge, not an amplification: no claim acquires a
stronger epistemic type by appearing in more than one paper, and consensus
among the drafts is never treated as proof.  We consolidate the definitions
into one three-layer object (compositional structure, generator, registered
interpretation), give a dependency ledger keyed to the companion papers'
claim identifiers, and prove four statements that are visible only across
papers: the additive constant of a generator is invisible to every registered
experiment (Proposition~\ref{prop:constant}); factor-level empirical adequacy
plus the monoidal product does not entail composite adequacy, with an
explicit tolerance witness (New result~\ref{thm:adequacy}); bare isomorphism and
$\varepsilon$-empirical equivalence are incomparable in the precise sense
supplied by HR's two counterexamples, with one residual gap recorded
(Proposition~\ref{prop:incomparable}); and no arrow class considered in the
series simultaneously preserves Hamiltonian structure, contains
coarse-graining, and composes totally (Proposition~\ref{prop:arrows}).  We
preserve, rather than resolve, three disagreements between the drafts and
four failed constructions.  A machine-checked Lean file and a type-checked
Haskell file accompany the paper; Section~\ref{sec:formal} states exactly
which claims they do and do not certify, and the answer is: none of the
smooth theorems.
\end{abstract}

\section{What this paper is, and what a synthesis may not do}
\label{sec:scope}

The pilot's founding thesis is that physics is the study of empirically
realized compositional mathematical structures.  All three companion papers
carry that sentence as a \emph{conjecture} and use it as a premise nowhere.
This paper does the same.

Three constraints govern what follows.  First, every displayed statement
carries exactly one epistemic type from the pilot contract: Definition,
Axiom, Assumption, Conjecture, Lemma, Proposition, Theorem, Corollary,
Interpretation, Empirical statement, Established physical result, or New
result claimed by this work.  Second, a claim imported from a companion paper
keeps the type it had there; agreement between two drafts is not evidence and
does not promote a conjecture to a theorem.  Third, where the drafts conflict,
or where a construction they attempt fails, the conflict and the failure are
recorded in Section~\ref{sec:tensions} rather than smoothed away.

Scope is inherited from the pilot charter: smooth, finite-dimensional, real
classical systems.  Field theory, infinite-dimensional analysis, general
relativity, and quantum theory appear only as boundary markers, and each such
appearance below is an Established physical result cited to its source or an
explicit statement that the present type stops there.  No new experimental
claim is made anywhere in this paper.

\section{The consolidated typed object}
\label{sec:object}

The three drafts define overlapping objects with different names.  We fix one
vocabulary and record the correspondence.

\begin{definition}[Three-layer object]
\label{def:layers}
A \emph{three-layer object} is a triple $(\mathcal{C}, \Th, \mathcal{I})$ in
which
\begin{enumerate}[label=(L\arabic*),leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item $\mathcal{C}$ is a symmetric monoidal category of systems and processes
      (CS Definitions of system, process, and composite; HR
      Definition of morphism and Cartesian product);
\item $\Th = (M,\omega,\mathcal{A}(M),H,\Phi)$ is a bare Hamiltonian object
      in $\mathcal{C}$: a symplectic manifold, its observable algebra, a
      generator, and the maximal local flow of the field $X_H$ determined by
      $\iota_{X_H}\omega = dH$ (HR Axioms on kinematic type, symplectic
      structure, observable type, generator, dynamical law);
\item $\mathcal{I} = (\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{ctx}}, K^P, K^Q, \varepsilon, D)$
      is a registered interpretation: experimental contexts, preparation and
      measurement kernels, a declared tolerance, and an intended domain (EL
      Definition of empirical interpretation; HR
      $\mathcal{I}=(P,\mu,\mathcal{U},\varepsilon,T)$ is the deterministic
      special case, augmented by units).
\end{enumerate}
Write $U(\mathcal{C},\Th,\mathcal{I}) = (\mathcal{C},\Th)$ for the forgetting
of layer (L3).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Predicted law, realization, discrepancy]
\label{def:law}
For a context $c\in D$ with control $u_c$ and duration $t_c$, the predicted
outcome law is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pred}
  \Pr_{\Th,\mathcal{I}}(A \mid c)
   = \int_M K^Q_c\bigl(A \mid \Phi^{u_c}_{t_c}(x)\bigr)\, K^P_c(dx).
\end{equation}
A physical target $S$ \emph{realizes} the object on $D$ to tolerance
$\varepsilon$ when $\TV\bigl(P^c_S, \Pr_{\Th,\mathcal{I}}(\cdot\mid c)\bigr)
\le \varepsilon(c)$ for every registered $c \in D$.  For two objects sharing
the registered outcome spaces,
$\Delta_D = \sup_{c\in D}\TV(P_1^c,P_2^c)$, and they are
$\eta$-observationally equivalent on $D$ when $\Delta_D \le \eta$.
\end{definition}

Definition~\ref{def:law} is EL's, transcribed.  Its role here is that
layers (L1) and (L2) never enter an empirical claim except through
\eqref{eq:pred}; that is the single channel through which mathematics reaches
data in this series, and every result below is a statement about what does or
does not pass through it.

\begin{axiom}[Typed equality]
\label{ax:typed}
States, processes, observables, interpretations, and empirical records are
distinct types.  An equality is admitted only between terms of one type; a
map relating two types is part of the data and is never suppressed.
\end{axiom}

Axiom~\ref{ax:typed} is CS's, and it is what makes
Proposition~\ref{prop:incomparable} statable rather than paradoxical.

\section{Results visible only across the three papers}
\label{sec:results}

Each result in this section uses inputs from at least two companion papers.

\subsection{The additive constant is empirically idle}

\begin{proposition}[Constant invisibility]
\label{prop:constant}
Let $(\mathcal{C},\Th,\mathcal{I})$ be a three-layer object with connected
$M$, and let $\Th_c$ be obtained by replacing $H$ with $H + c$ for a constant
$c\in\Rr$.  Then for every registered context set $D$ and every choice of
kernels in $\mathcal{I}$,
\[
  \Pr_{\Th,\mathcal{I}}(\,\cdot \mid c') = \Pr_{\Th_c,\mathcal{I}}(\,\cdot\mid c')
  \quad\text{for all } c' \in D,
  \qquad\text{hence}\qquad \Delta_D(\Th,\Th_c) = 0 .
\]
Conversely, if $\Th'=(M,\omega,\mathcal{A}(M),H',\Phi')$ has $\Phi'=\Phi$ on a
neighbourhood of $t=0$, then $H' = H + c$ for some constant $c$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By HR lem-hamiltonian-field, $X_{H+c}$ is the unique field with
$\iota_{X_{H+c}}\omega = d(H+c) = dH$, so $X_{H+c} = X_H$, and two vector
fields that are equal have the same maximal local flow: $\Phi^{H+c}=\Phi^H$.
Both sides of \eqref{eq:pred} then integrate the same kernel against the same
pushforward, so the predicted laws agree context by context, and the supremum
of a family of zeros is zero.  For the converse, equality of the flows near
$t=0$ gives equality of their generators' fields by differentiation at
$t=0$, and HR cor-underdetermination on connected $M$ gives $H'-H$ locally
constant, hence constant.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The observational map is not injective]
\label{cor:noninjective}
The assignment sending a bare object to its observational class on $D$ is not
injective on generators: it identifies $H$ with $H+c$ for every $c$.  No
enlargement of $D$ within Definition~\ref{def:law} separates them.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:constant} gives $\Delta_D = 0$ for every $D$, and the
EL restriction lemma states only that shrinking $D$ cannot increase
$\Delta$; enlarging $D$ leaves the computation of \eqref{eq:pred} unchanged
because the flows are equal.
\end{proof}

\begin{interpretation}[Zero of energy]
\label{int:zero}
Within this scope an absolute zero of energy is not an empirically realized
element of the object: it is a coordinate on the space of generators that
\eqref{eq:pred} annihilates.  HR records the same reading at
int-energy-zero.  That gravitation changes the situation is a boundary fact,
outside the present type, and is not used.
\end{interpretation}

\subsection{Factor adequacy does not compose}

CS supplies the counterexample that interaction defeats product dynamics; HR
sharpens it to an exact characterisation of the range of $\otimes$; EL
supplies the tolerance language in which ``adequate'' is defined.  Only the
combination states what an experimenter would need.

\begin{assumption}[Registered isolated adequacy]
\label{as:isolated}
Let the target be a pair of laboratory oscillators that can be operated
singly.  We assume: (i) for the isolated contexts $D_1$ (one subsystem
prepared and read, the other absent), the uncoupled objects
$\Th_i = (T^*\Rr, dq_i\wedge dp_i, H_i)$ with $H_i = (p_i^2+q_i^2)/2$ realize
the target exactly, with noiseless readout $\mu(z)=q_1$ in metres; (ii) the
joint contexts $D_{12}$ prepare both subsystems together.  Clause (i) is an
empirical premise, not a theorem, and it is chosen exact only to isolate the
compositional failure from measurement error.
\end{assumption}

\begin{newresult}[Composite adequacy is not inherited]
\label{thm:adequacy}
Adopt Assumption~\ref{as:isolated}, and let the joint target be governed by
\[
  H^{\kappa} = \tfrac12(p_1^2+p_2^2) + \tfrac12(q_1^2+q_2^2)
             + \tfrac{\kappa}{2}(q_1-q_2)^2, \qquad \kappa>0 ,
\]
on $(T^*\Rr)^{\otimes 2}$ with the split form.  Let $\Th_1\otimes\Th_2$ be the
monoidal composite of the two factor objects.  Then for the joint context that
prepares $z_0=(q_1,q_2,p_1,p_2)=(1,-1,0,0)$ and reads $\mu = q_1$ at time
$t_\kappa := \pi/\sqrt{1+2\kappa}$,
\[
  \bigl|\mu(\Phi^{\kappa}_{t_\kappa} z_0) - \mu\bigl((\Phi^{1}\times\Phi^{2})_{t_\kappa} z_0\bigr)\bigr|
  \;=\; 1 + \cos\!\Bigl(\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{1+2\kappa}}\Bigr) \;=:\; \delta(\kappa) \;>\; 0 .
\]
The coordinate separation is $\delta(\kappa)$, whereas the total-variation
discrepancy of the noiseless outcome laws is exactly $1$. Consequently, for
every total-variation tolerance $\varepsilon < 1$ and every
window $T \ge t_\kappa$, the factor objects realize the target on $D_1$ while
$\Th_1\otimes\Th_2$ fails to realize it on $D_{12}$.  Moreover
$\delta(\kappa)\to 0$ as $\kappa\downarrow 0$ and $\delta(\kappa)\to 2$ as
$\kappa\to\infty$.
\end{newresult}

\begin{proof}
Set $u=(q_1+q_2)/\sqrt2$, $v=(q_1-q_2)/\sqrt2$ and correspondingly for the
momenta; this is a linear symplectic change of coordinates, so the split form
is preserved and
$H^{\kappa} = \tfrac12(p_u^2+p_v^2) + \tfrac12 u^2 + \tfrac12(1+2\kappa)v^2$.
The two normal modes are uncoupled harmonic oscillators with frequencies
$\omega_+=1$ and $\omega_-=\sqrt{1+2\kappa}$.  The initial datum $z_0$ has
$u=0$, $p_u=0$, $v=\sqrt2$, $p_v=0$, so $u(t)\equiv 0$ and
$v(t)=\sqrt2\cos(\omega_- t)$, whence $q_1(t) = \cos(\omega_- t)$.

By CS-T1 (equivalently HR prop-product-well-typed), the composite
$\Th_1\otimes\Th_2$ has generator $H_1\circ\pi_1 + H_2\circ\pi_2$ and flow
$\Phi^1_t\times\Phi^2_t$, so its prediction for the same datum is
$q_1(t)=\cos t$.  At $t_\kappa = \pi/\omega_-$ the target value is
$\cos\pi = -1$ and the composite's prediction is $\cos(\pi/\omega_-)$.  Since
$\kappa>0$ gives $\omega_->1$, we have $0 < \pi/\omega_- < \pi$ and therefore
$\cos(\pi/\omega_-) > -1$; the absolute difference is
$1+\cos(\pi/\omega_-) = \delta(\kappa) > 0$.  Thus the predicted point masses
are distinct. Under Definition~\ref{def:law}, their total-variation distance
is exactly $1$, so realization fails precisely for tolerances $\varepsilon<1$.
The limits stated for the separate coordinate diagnostic follow by continuity of
$\kappa\mapsto 1+\cos(\pi/\sqrt{1+2\kappa})$ at the endpoints.
\end{proof}

\begin{interpretation}[Reading of New result~\ref{thm:adequacy}]
\label{int:adequacy}
The mathematics here is a quantified restatement of HR
new-interaction-outside-tensor: because $\kappa\ne0$ makes the coupling
non-constant, the target's flow is outside the range of $\otimes$, and the
only contribution of this paper is the explicit coordinate diagnostic
$\delta(\kappa)$ and its separation from the total-variation discrepancy.
Under noiseless readout the latter jumps from $0$ to $1$ for every nonzero
coupling; it does not inherit the smooth small-coupling behavior of
$\delta(\kappa)$. Two
readings must be resisted.  It does not show that composition fails in
general. EL's compositional error bound remains a conditional positive result
under its independence assumption, but this noiseless TV example supplies no
small-error regime. It does show that the assumption is load-bearing, and that reporting factor
adequacy without an interface test reports nothing about the composite.
\end{interpretation}

\subsection{Isomorphism and empirical equivalence are incomparable}

\begin{proposition}[Incomparability, with a recorded residual]
\label{prop:incomparable}
Consider bare isomorphism $\cong$ (HR Definition of morphism) and
$\varepsilon$-empirical equivalence $\sim_\varepsilon$ on $(P,T)$
(HR Definition of $\varepsilon$-empirical equivalence).  Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item $\sim_\varepsilon$ does not imply $\cong$.  Witness: the harmonic object
      and its quartic perturbation $H_\lambda = H_0 + \lambda q^4$ for
      $\lambda$ small (HR prop-equivalent-not-isomorphic).
\item $\cong$ does not imply identity of empirical models.  Witness: the
      mass--spring and $LC$ objects with $m=L$, $k=1/C$, whose bare objects
      are equal and whose empirical models are records in metres and in
      coulombs respectively (HR prop-isomorphic-not-physically-equivalent).
\end{enumerate}
The two relations are therefore incomparable in this precise sense.  Residual:
(ii) exhibits objects whose empirical models are not comparable without a
translation of units, not two objects with a common registered outcome space
and different laws.  Whether $\cong$ can fail to imply $\sim_\varepsilon$
\emph{after} a fixed unit translation and a common registered outcome space is
not settled by the series, and is recorded as
Open problem~\ref{op:iso}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
(i) HR proves a $\lambda$-independent Gr\"onwall bound
$\sup_{z\in P}\sup_{t\in[0,T]}\|\Phi^\lambda_t z-\Phi^0_t z\|\le C\lambda$ and,
separately, that the period function of $H_\lambda$ is non-constant while that
of $H_0$ is constant, which blocks any isomorphism because isomorphisms
intertwine flows and hence preserve periods.  Choosing $\lambda<\varepsilon/C$
gives $\sim_\varepsilon$ without $\cong$.
(ii) HR exhibits the identity of $\Rr^2$ as an isomorphism of bare objects and
observes that $U$ deletes the unit assignment $\mathcal{U}$, so no bare
isomorphism induces a map of empirical models.  By Axiom~\ref{ax:typed} the
records are of different types, so the equality of empirical models is not
merely false but ill-typed.  Combining the two witnesses gives
incomparability.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[No arrow class in the series does all three jobs]
\label{prop:arrows}
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be one of the three arrow classes considered in the series:
(a) all smooth maps (CS's $\Ham_{\rm kin}$); (b) maps with
$\varphi^*\omega'=\omega$ and $H=H'\circ\varphi+c$ (HR's morphisms);
(c) canonical relations.  Then no one of them has all three properties:
preservation of Hamiltonian structure, containment of a dimension-decreasing
(coarse-graining) arrow, and totally defined composition.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Class (a) contains the dilation $f(q,p)=(2q,p)$, for which
$f^*(dq\wedge dp) = 2\,dq\wedge dp \ne dq\wedge dp$ (CS counterexample), so
it fails preservation.  Class (b) satisfies preservation by definition, and
by HR lem-symplectic-immersion every arrow is an immersion, so
$\dim M\le\dim M'$ and HR new-no-coarse-graining excludes dimension decrease;
it fails containment.  Class (c) contains reductions but, by HR
est-weinstein-not-category, the set-theoretic composite of two canonical
relations need not be a submanifold and need not be Lagrangian when it is, so
composition is partial; it fails total composability.
\end{proof}

\begin{openproblem}
\label{op:arrows}
Proposition~\ref{prop:arrows} is a statement about the three classes the
series considers, not a proof that no class succeeds.  Whether some class of
arrows --- spans, decorated cospans, or the loose arrows of a double category
--- achieves all three properties simultaneously for finite-dimensional
symplectic objects is open, and is the content of HR
conj-interconnection-completeness.
\end{openproblem}

\begin{openproblem}
\label{op:iso}
Do there exist two theory objects with isomorphic bare objects, a common
registered outcome space, a fixed unit translation, and
$\Delta_D>\varepsilon$?  A positive answer would strengthen
Proposition~\ref{prop:incomparable}(ii) from a typing obstruction to a
measured discrepancy; a negative answer would show that after unit
translation isomorphism does imply empirical equivalence within this scope.
\end{openproblem}

\section{Preserved disagreements and failed constructions}
\label{sec:tensions}

A synthesis that reported only the compatible parts would misrepresent the
series.  Four failures and three tensions are recorded.  None is resolved
here.

\paragraph{Tension 1: the compositional verdict.}
CS concludes with a layered positive result --- symmetric monoidal kinematics
proved, factorized dynamics proved for separable generators, physical
compositionality left to empirical assumption.  HR concludes that the
compositional reading is \emph{incomplete in a provable way}: $\otimes$ cannot
express interaction, the richer composition is partial, and the category has
no coarse-graining arrows.  These are logically compatible --- CS proves that
a monoidal structure exists, HR computes the range of that structure --- but
they support different summaries, and the difference matters for the thesis.
The consolidated position taken here: the existence claim is proved (CS-P1),
the sufficiency claim is refuted for $\otimes$ alone (HR
new-interaction-outside-tensor), and New result~\ref{thm:adequacy} shows the
refutation survives translation into tolerance language.

\paragraph{Tension 2: which arrows are the physics.}
CS admits all smooth maps as processes, so that preparation and readout maps
exist, and then must add a counterexample showing such arrows preserve
neither the symplectic form nor Poisson brackets.  HR admits only
form-preserving maps and then must record that coarse-graining leaves the
category.  Proposition~\ref{prop:arrows} states the cost of each choice.  The
series does not select between them, and this synthesis does not either.

\paragraph{Tension 3: metadata integrity of the series.}
The round-four series review returned verdict \texttt{MAJOR} with five
blocking findings, all of them about the correspondence between each paper's
LaTeX and its machine-readable claim list: CS exported 7 claims against 27
epistemic environments in the source; HR exported 44 against 53; EL exported
5, with dependency fields containing free text (for example ``Definitions of
empirical interpretation and indexed observational equivalence'') instead of
claim identifiers; and CS-T1 depends on an identifier CS-L1 that its own
metadata omits.  This synthesis repairs the ledger only for the claims it
uses (Table~\ref{tab:ledger}, where every dependency is an identifier or a
named established result).  It does not repair the companion papers' own
metadata, and the review's finding stands against them.

\paragraph{Failed construction 1: the symplectic category.}
Objects symplectic manifolds, arrows canonical relations, is the natural
repair for the expressive deficit of $\otimes$.  It is not a category: the
composite of two canonical relations need not be a submanifold, and need not
be Lagrangian when it is (HR est-weinstein-not-category).  Transversality or
clean-intersection hypotheses restore composition only partially.

\paragraph{Failed construction 2: a quantization functor.}
No linear, unit-preserving map from the Poisson algebra of polynomials on
$\Rr^{2n}$ to self-adjoint operators sends $\{f,g\}$ to
$\frac{1}{i\hbar}[\hat f,\hat g]$ and acts irreducibly on linear polynomials
(HR est-groenewold-van-hove).  The obstruction is a theorem, so the typed
object does not extend functorially past its boundary.  Quantum theory is a
boundary marker for this series and nothing is claimed inside it.

\paragraph{Failed construction 3: dissipation inside the type.}
A globally asymptotically stable planar field is Hamiltonian for \emph{no}
symplectic form (HR new-damped-no-symplectic-form), so no change of geometry
rescues damped systems within the type on that domain.  Time-dependent or
restricted-domain presentations exist and are standard; they are different
objects.

\paragraph{Failed construction 4: formalizing the smooth theorems.}
The intended formal appendix for this series was a machine-checked
verification of its central results.  That was not achieved and is not
claimed.  What was achieved is described next.

\label{int:lean}
It certifies six discrete surrogates and nothing else.  In the surrogate a
manifold is a bare type, a symplectic form is absent, a vector field is a step
function, and the first de Rham cohomology of the circle is replaced by the
cycle sum of a periodic integer sequence.  The checked statements are: the run
of a monoidal product factors into the runs of its factors; every product is
non-interacting and an explicitly coupled system is therefore not a product;
a coupling is additively separable exactly when its mixed second difference
vanishes; a difference operator determines its potential up to an additive
constant; a periodic form admits a global potential exactly when its cycle sum
vanishes, with the constant form on an $n$-cycle as an explicit obstruction;
and a property is identifiable exactly when it factors through the
observational quotient.  These are surrogates of CS-P1, HR
new-interaction-outside-tensor, CS-N1, HR cor-underdetermination, HR
thm-exact-sequence with its torus non-example, and EL C2, respectively.  A
surrogate that checks does not certify the smooth statement.  Nothing in the
file mentions a manifold, a $2$-form, a flow, a probability kernel, or a
tolerance, so no theorem of the series is verified by it.
\end{interpretation}

\begin{interpretation}[What the Haskell file certifies]
\label{int:haskell}
Less.  Its type declarations render Definition~\ref{def:layers} and
Definition~\ref{def:law} as types, which fixes arities and dependencies and
makes the deletion performed by $U$ visible: units are a field of the
interpretation record and are unreachable from the bare object.  Its checks
are of two kinds, marked in the source.  Those marked \texttt{DEC} are exact
on the finite data supplied --- separability on a grid, cycle sums,
observational classes of a three-element model class --- and their
correctness criteria are the statements proved in the Lean file.  Those marked
\texttt{NUM} are floating-point illustrations with untracked discretisation
and rounding error: RK4 trajectories exhibiting the Gr\"onwall-type
separation of Proposition~\ref{prop:incomparable}(i), the decay of the damped
field, and total-variation arithmetic.  A \texttt{NUM} check that passes is
an illustration and is not evidence.
\end{interpretation}

\begin{openproblem}
\label{op:formal}
Formalize, in a proof assistant with a differential-geometry library, at least
HR lem-hamiltonian-field and HR cor-underdetermination, and then
Proposition~\ref{prop:constant} on top of them.  The surrogate development
here indicates only that the combinatorial skeleton is consistent; the smooth
content --- nondegeneracy, Cartan's formula, the Poincar\'e lemma --- is
exactly what the surrogate omits.
\end{openproblem}

\section{Falsification conditions for the synthesised object}
\label{sec:falsification}

\begin{definition}[Refutation of the type on a domain]
\label{def:refute}
The three-layer type is refuted for a domain $D$ of physical systems when
there are a system in $D$, a registered interpretation, and recorded data
$\mathcal{D}$ such that no object satisfying the HR axioms on any
finite-dimensional $M$, with any registered interpretation, satisfies
Definition~\ref{def:law}'s realization criterion for $\mathcal{D}$.
\end{definition}

Four registered targets, stated as targets and not as claims, and each with
the companion result that makes it discriminating:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(F\arabic*)},leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \emph{Bracket failure}: measured response functions of a closed
      finite-dimensional system whose induced bracket violates the Jacobi
      identity beyond tolerance.  This refutes $d\omega=0$ for that system
      (HR lem-poisson-identities).
\item \emph{Global generator failure}: measured evolution preserving $\omega$
      with a measurably nonzero class $[\iota_X\omega]\in
      H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)$.  This refutes the dynamical-law axiom while
      leaving the kinematic axioms intact (HR cor-underdetermination; the
      Lean file's cycle-sum obstruction is the discrete shadow of this test).
\item \emph{Irreducible dissipation}: a closed finite-dimensional system with
      a globally asymptotically stable equilibrium.  By HR
      new-damped-no-symplectic-form this refutes the dynamical law under
      \emph{every} symplectic form on its state space.
\item \emph{Interface failure}: two subsystems each realized to tolerance
      $\varepsilon$ on isolated contexts whose joint records exceed
      $\varepsilon$ on joint contexts by more than the EL compositional
      bound permits.  By New result~\ref{thm:adequacy} this refutes the
      independence assumption rather than the type, which is what makes it a
      diagnostic rather than a global test.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{empirical}[Evidential status]
\label{emp:status}
None of (F1)--(F4) is reported as observed here; no new measurement is
presented in this paper.  The series' only empirical anchor is the cited
adequacy of laboratory mass--spring and $LC$ objects within stated regimes
(HR emp-laboratory-anchor), and its only cited refutation of a specific
object is the perihelion advance of Mercury against the two-body Newtonian
Hamiltonian~\cite{Will2014}, which refutes that object and not the type.
\end{empirical}

\section{Open conjectures carried forward}

\begin{conjecture}[Interconnection completeness; HR]
\label{conj:interconnection}
There is a symmetric monoidal double category whose loose arrows are
partially-defined canonical relations and whose tight arrows are
symplectomorphisms, in which every object
$(M_1\times M_2,\ \omega_1\oplus\omega_2,\ H_1\oplus H_2 + V)$ arises as a
composite of the factors and one arrow determined by $V$.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Compositional empirical adequacy; EL]
\label{conj:adequacy}
For a useful class of finite-dimensional classical laboratory systems,
interpretations and error budgets can be assigned compositionally so that
subsystem adequacy plus verified interface assumptions entails system-level
adequacy with nonvacuous bounds.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Joint form; this paper]
\label{conj:joint}
Conjectures~\ref{conj:interconnection} and \ref{conj:adequacy} are the
mathematical and the empirical halves of one statement: that the
interconnection arrow determined by a coupling $V$ can be assigned an error
budget depending only on $V$ and on the interface, in such a way that
New result~\ref{thm:adequacy}'s $\delta(\kappa)$ is recovered as the special
case of a quadratic coupling.  We do not assert this; it is offered as the
shape a positive result would have to take, and it inherits every obstruction
recorded in Section~\ref{sec:tensions}.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Empirical bite of symplectic rigidity; HR]
\label{conj:rigidity}
There exist a finite-dimensional classical laboratory system, an
interpretation, and two candidate objects agreeing on all volume-theoretic
predictions within tolerance whose readout predictions differ by more than the
tolerance solely as a consequence of Gromov non-squeezing.  No such
realization is known to the series, and none is asserted.
\end{conjecture}

\section{Assessment}

\begin{interpretation}[Status of the founding thesis after synthesis]
\label{int:status}
The bounded report the three papers jointly support, and nothing stronger.
\emph{Precision:} for finite-dimensional classical mechanics the thesis is
statable, by Definitions~\ref{def:layers} and \ref{def:law}.
\emph{Internal coherence:} the object reproduces the standard formalism (HR
thm-local-reconstruction, thm-exact-sequence) without modification.
\emph{Usefulness:} the typing did work; it produced HR's three new results,
CS's separation equivalence, EL's error-transfer theorem, and the four
cross-paper statements of Section~\ref{sec:results}, and it converted the
methodological preference for interconnection over tensor into a theorem
about the range of $\otimes$.
\emph{Costs, named:} $\otimes$ cannot express interaction; the natural richer
composition is only partially defined; the structure-preserving arrow class
contains no coarse-graining; the type does not extend functorially to quantum
theory; and its formal appendix verifies discrete surrogates only.
\emph{Verdict:} we do not report the thesis as established.  We report that,
in this domain, it is precise, non-trivial, partially falsifiable by
(F1)--(F4), and encumbered by five named deficits.  Realization remains what
CS-I1, HR int-locus and EL's realization criterion each independently make it:
empirical input, never a consequence of mathematical admissibility.
\end{interpretation}
